Privacy Policy for Pi Puzzle

Effective Date: November 2026

Welcome to Pi Puzzle. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information related to your use of our application within the Pi Network ecosystem.

1. Information We Collect

Pi Puzzle is a casual puzzle game. We do not collect, store, or share any sensitive personal information, private keys, or passphrases. If you connect using the Pi SDK, we only access your public Pi username solely for game functionality and leaderboard purposes.

2. How We Use Information

Any basic gameplay data (such as high scores or game progress) is used strictly to enhance your gaming experience and ensure the proper functioning of the app.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app operates within the Pi Browser and interacts with official Pi Network APIs. Please refer to Pi Network's official privacy policy for information on how they handle platform-level data.

4. Data Security

We implement standard security measures to keep your game data safe. We never sell or rent user data to third parties.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach us through our official GitHub repository: pipuzzle2026.github.io.
